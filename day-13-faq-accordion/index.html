Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about booking a shoot? We've got answers.

We recommend booking at least 1 to 2 weeks in advance, especially for holiday events like Tobaski or weekend portrait slots, to secure your preferred time.

Our Essential Package covers 1 look, Signature Glam includes up to 2 outfit looks, and Ultimate Luxe permits unlimited outfit changes within your session time.

High-resolution retouched photos are delivered via a private Google Drive link within 48 to 72 hours following your session.

Raw photos are included exclusively with our Ultimate Luxe package, or can be added onto any standard package for a minor additional fee.
