Booking Terms & Policy Details

Captured by CeeTrio — Official Guidelines

  1. Advance Booking: Bookings must be made 2–3 days in advance.
  2. Deposit Required: 50% deposit is required to reserve a slot.
  3. Agreement Confirmation: Booking is confirmed only after DM agreement and deposit receipt.
  4. Punctuality: Clients must arrive on time (10–20 min grace period). Late arrival may reduce shoot time.
  5. Editing & Delivery: Editing takes 2–3 days depending on the shoot. Photos are delivered via WhatsApp or Google Drive. RAW/unedited photos are not provided.
  6. Styling & Preparation: Clients are responsible for their own outfits and preparation.
  7. Transportation: Transportation fee is already included in all package charges.
  8. Group Shoots: For group photoshoots, pricing and arrangements may vary depending on the number of participants.
  9. Rescheduling & Cancellation: Rescheduling must be done 24 hours before. Last-minute cancellations forfeit deposit.
  10. Respectful Conduct: Respectful communication is required at all times.
